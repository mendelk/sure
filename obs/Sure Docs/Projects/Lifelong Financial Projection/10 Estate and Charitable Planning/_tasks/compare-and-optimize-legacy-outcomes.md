Make legacy a first-class objective when comparing plans, withdrawal orders, conversions, and charitable strategies.

## Acceptance criteria
- Comparisons show gross estate, legacy, net legacy, heirs, charity, estate drag, taxes, costs, and account composition.
- Tax strategy analysis captures how Roth conversions and gain harvesting affect heirs rather than only the plan owner's lifetime taxes.
- Chance-of-success can report percentile distributions for net legacy and beneficiary outcomes.
- Optimization can target net legacy or heirs' amount subject to spending, giving, tax, and solvency constraints.
- Tradeoffs between current consumption, lifetime giving, tax savings, and final bequests remain explicit.

## Verification
- Compare baseline, conversion, charitable, alternate drawdown, and survivor scenarios under deterministic and stochastic runs.
