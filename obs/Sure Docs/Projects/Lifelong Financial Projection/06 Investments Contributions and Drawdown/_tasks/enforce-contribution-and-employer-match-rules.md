Apply account-, person-, age-, income-, and year-specific contribution rules while preserving flexible flow strategies.

## Acceptance criteria
- Employee and employer contributions support percentages, fixed amounts, tiered matches, fixed mandatory contributions, and Traditional/Roth designation.
- Limits include employee, employer, combined, catch-up, income phaseouts, family/individual HSA, IRA, 529, and custom jurisdiction rules.
- Backdoor Roth and mega backdoor Roth support after-tax space, fixed/max contributions, in-plan conversion, and Roth IRA routing.
- Defined-contribution account variants retain distinct legal rules instead of collapsing into a generic retirement account.
- Exceeded or unavailable contribution room is reported and lower-priority flows receive unused cash.

## Verification
- Annual fixtures cover age catch-up, job change, couples, employer match, income phaseout, and partial-year contribution history.
