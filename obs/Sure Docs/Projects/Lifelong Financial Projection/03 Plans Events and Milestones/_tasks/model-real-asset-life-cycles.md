Model homes, rentals, vehicles, businesses, and custom real assets from purchase through operation, financing, sale, or estate settlement.

## Acceptance criteria
- Purchases support cash or financed funding, down payment, loan terms, purchase/sale costs, ownership, and source accounts.
- Value, assessed value, rent, maintenance, insurance, HOA, and property tax can grow independently by amount, asset percentage, inflation relationship, or custom schedule.
- Rental income supports vacancy, expenses, depreciation-relevant basis, personal-use share, and tax classification.
- Sales model proceeds, debt payoff, capital gains exclusions, taxes, destination accounts, and multiple sales in one year.
- Assets can recur with stop conditions/scaling and can auto-liquidate only when required by liquidity and configured drawdown order.

## Verification
- Cover rent-versus-buy, primary residence, rental property, house hacking, recurring vehicle replacement, and emergency liquidation.
