Let users learn projection concepts safely through fictional plans and contextual explanations.

## Acceptance criteria
- Sandbox mode uses an isolated fictional persona and never mixes sample activity with household finances.
- Templates cover early career, mid-career, late career, FIRE, couples, home purchase, and retirement drawdown.
- A sandbox can be customized, discarded, or converted into a clean real plan only through an explicit mapping step.
- Searchable guidance explains accounts, cash flow, milestones, projections, tax, investments, estate, progress, and troubleshooting.
- Product tours and explanations can be disabled and revisited without affecting plan data.

## Verification
- Every advanced capability links to a definition, assumptions, worked example, or documented limitation.
