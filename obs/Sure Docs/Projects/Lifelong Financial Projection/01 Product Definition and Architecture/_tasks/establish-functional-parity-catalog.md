Create and maintain a comprehensive catalog of personal and household financial-planning capabilities. Treat the catalog as an outcome target rather than a prescription for interface, terminology, defaults, branding, or commercial structure.

## Acceptance criteria
- Every publicly documented planning capability is mapped to a task, explicitly deferred with rationale, or identified as already supported by Sure.
- The catalog distinguishes household planning, simulation, tax, investment, analytics, estate, international, security, API, and portability capabilities.
- Each entry records its source URL, expected user outcome, important variants, and parity status.
- Changelog-only details such as block bootstrap, inherited IRAs, withdrawal shielding, gain harvesting, flex spending, and estate drag are included.
- Advisor, employer, subscription, billing, gift, affiliate, and directory features are explicitly marked out of scope rather than represented as parity gaps.
- The catalog can be rerun against later releases without losing prior decisions.

## Verification
- Review the catalog against the approved capability inventory and record every omission or deferral with rationale.
