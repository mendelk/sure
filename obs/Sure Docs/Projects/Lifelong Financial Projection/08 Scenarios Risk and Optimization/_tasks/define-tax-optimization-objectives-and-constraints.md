Define optimization targets, annual ceilings, allowed actions, time ranges, and non-negotiable plan constraints.

## Acceptance criteria
- Targets include bracket, IRMAA, ACA/FPL, taxable income, MAGI, taxes, net worth, and net legacy.
- Constraints include solvency, spending, account availability, basis, filing, contribution limits, and user ceilings.
- Conflicting or impossible objective sets fail with an explanation before search begins.

## Verification
- Validate feasible and infeasible configurations around known tax cliffs.
