Let users see and change where annual surplus goes instead of assuming one universal savings rate.

## Ownership boundary
Add an ordered allocation experiment to the current calculation only; do not design a generalized rules engine.

## Acceptance criteria
- Route surplus through visible priorities for required spending, debt, goals, and contributions.
- Enforce simple contribution and employer-match limits for the supported retirement accounts.
- Keep transfers separate from income, spending, and investment growth.
- Explain unused cash and unmet allocation targets each year.

## Verification
- Exercise competing debt, employer match, savings, and goal priorities and reconcile every allocated dollar.
