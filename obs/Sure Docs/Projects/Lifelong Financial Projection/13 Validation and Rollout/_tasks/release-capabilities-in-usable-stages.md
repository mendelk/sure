Sequence delivery so every release provides a coherent planning workflow rather than isolated calculators or unusable model fragments.

## Acceptance criteria
- Stage 1 delivers current snapshot, plans, core events/flows, deterministic projection, yearly summary, and progress.
- Stage 2 adds investments, contribution/drawdown rules, US tax/benefits, compare, historical backtests, and reporting.
- Stage 3 adds Monte Carlo, flex spending, advanced retirement/tax strategies, optimization, and estate planning.
- Stage 4 expands international presets, API/plugin coverage, portability, and final planning breadth parity.
- Each stage defines migration, data portability, documentation, calculation validation, observability, and opt-out/rollback expectations.

## Verification
- A representative user can complete a meaningful end-to-end plan at every stage without depending on unreleased functionality.
