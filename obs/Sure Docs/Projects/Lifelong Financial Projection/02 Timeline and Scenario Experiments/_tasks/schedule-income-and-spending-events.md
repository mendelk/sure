Let users replace flat income and spending with visible recurring and dated life events.

## Ownership boundary
Own simple local event editing and projection application. Do not build a generic rule language or event inheritance system.

## Acceptance criteria
- Add, edit, disable, and remove recurring income and spending events.
- Support start/end dates, annual changes, and one-time amounts.
- Assign an event to the household or one member and show its active years.
- Recalculate the projection and yearly explanation from the edited events.

## Verification
- Create salary, retirement, recurring living cost, and one-time expense events and verify their first, last, and inactive years.
