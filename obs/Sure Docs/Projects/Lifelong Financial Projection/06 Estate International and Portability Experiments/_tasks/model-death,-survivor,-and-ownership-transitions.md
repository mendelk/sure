Show how a household plan changes when one member dies, including ownership, income, taxes, healthcare, and goals.

## Ownership boundary
Implement transitions in selected reference plans first. Avoid a universal estate entity model.

## Acceptance criteria
- End person-specific income, spending, benefits, and events at death.
- Transfer or terminate supported accounts and real assets according to explicit ownership and beneficiary assumptions.
- Apply supported survivor benefit, filing, healthcare, and spending changes.
- Keep unsupported legal and ownership cases visible with conservative manual overrides.

## Verification
- Exercise first-death and second-death years for a couple and reconcile ownership, cash flow, taxes, benefits, and goals.
